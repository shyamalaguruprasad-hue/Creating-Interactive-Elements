Segment and Build Audiences

Three short, hands-on activities for Short Course 2. Each one turns an idea from the readings into something you decide, sort or build, with instant feedback.

Ungraded practice. Works on desktop and phone, and each activity has a sound on/off button.

Module 1: Create buyer personas

Practice after the reading on synthesizing data sources.

Persona evidence board

Sort 16 pieces of CRM, LinkedIn and interview evidence into a persona canvas, leave out the noise, and watch your persona take shape.

Module 2: Define and apply segmentation

Practice before and during the Excel segmentation table activity.

Segment size simulator

You will: balance relevance against reachAbout 6 minutes
Open the simulator

Segmentation table audit

The launch is tomorrow. Pick up the red pen and circle every problem hiding in a colleague’s segmentation table, then see the fixes.

You will: spot flawed criteria, logic and sizingAbout 8 minutes
Open the audit

Before you start

  • There are no scores that count. Try things, get it wrong, and read the feedback.
  • You can use a mouse, a finger or the keyboard. Select an item, then select where it goes.
  • Contact counts and company details are made up for practice.
  • Turn sound off with the Sound button inside any activity if you are in a shared space.
